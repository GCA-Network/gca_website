# Institution logos

The banner on the home page looks for a logo per institution and falls back to
a text label when it does not find one. The institution must also be listed in
the `INSTITUTIONS` array in `public/index.html`.

Naming: `<slug>.png` is tried first, then `<slug>.svg`.
SVG is preferred; PNG should be transparent and at least 300px on its long side.
Plates are a fixed 206x66, and logos are capped at 158x38 inside them. Prefer a
readable wordmark or abbreviation over an emblem-only mark.
